we care about you!

High-quality milk plays a key role in producing delicious ice cream. It is important that the milk is fresh and natural, without any additives. Well-chosen milk gives the ice cream a delicate texture and rich flavor.

We work with reliable milk suppliers to ensure consistently high product quality. We also adhere to strict hygiene and safety standards at all stages of production, which ensures the safety and quality of the finished ice cream.

The company uses various types of plant-based milk to produce ice cream to cater to diverse consumer preferences and provide an alternative to traditional dairy ice cream. Using plant-based milk allows for the creation of different flavors and textures, meeting the needs of those who avoid dairy products or seek new and more interesting ice cream options.
